Cipher Analysis Workbench

A message investigation tool that encrypts and decrypts text with Vigenère, AES-256-GCM, and Rail Fence. It accepts typed text and CSV/TXT uploads, preserves CSV records and MessageIDs, reports processing status, exports results, and compares A–Z letter frequencies before and after Vigenère encryption.
